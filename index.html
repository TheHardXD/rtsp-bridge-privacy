# RTSP Bridge Privacy Policy **Effective date:** September 23, 2026 **Publisher:** Tytus Borycki **Contact:** [tytusborycki@gmail.com](mailto:tytusborycki@gmail.com) ## 1. General information This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed by **RTSP Bridge**. RTSP Bridge is designed to receive RTSP video streams from network cameras and expose them as virtual camera devices in Windows, for use with applications such as Zoom. The application is designed so that camera video processing takes place primarily on the user’s local device. ## 2. Data controller The data controller for information related to the operation and support of RTSP Bridge is: **Tytus Borycki** Email: **[tytusborycki@gmail.com](mailto:tytusborycki@gmail.com)** For privacy, personal data, security, or application-related inquiries, you may contact the publisher using the email address above. ## 3. Information the application may process Depending on how RTSP Bridge is used, the application may access or process: * IP addresses or host names of network cameras, * RTSP URLs, * usernames and passwords required to connect to cameras, * technical camera information such as manufacturer, model, stream profiles, and supported video parameters, * live video received from RTSP streams, * names and configuration of saved devices, * application settings, * subscription and entitlement information provided by Microsoft Store, * usage information related to subscription limits, where applicable, * diagnostic information voluntarily provided by the user when reporting a problem. ## 4. Camera video RTSP camera video is processed locally on the user’s computer in order to create a virtual Windows camera. RTSP Bridge does not transmit camera video to servers operated by the publisher. RTSP Bridge does not automatically record or archive camera video. If the user uses RTSP Bridge together with third-party software such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or another application that receives video from the virtual camera, any further processing performed by that third-party application is governed by its own privacy policy. ## 5. Camera credentials RTSP Bridge may store information required to reconnect to saved cameras, including RTSP addresses and authentication credentials. This information is stored locally on the user’s device. The application uses security mechanisms provided by Windows to protect stored authentication information. The publisher does not receive or store passwords to the user’s cameras. Users are responsible for securing their own local network, devices, and camera credentials. ## 6. Local network device discovery If the user enables automatic camera discovery, RTSP Bridge may send local network discovery requests using technologies such as ONVIF or WS-Discovery. Information obtained through local discovery is used only to identify and configure devices available on the user’s network. This information is not automatically transmitted to the publisher. ## 7. Subscriptions and payments RTSP Bridge subscriptions are handled through **Microsoft Store**. RTSP Bridge may receive information from Microsoft Store that is necessary to determine whether the user has an active subscription and which subscription tier is active. The publisher does not receive the user’s payment card details or other payment credentials. Payments, renewals, cancellations, billing, and subscription management are handled according to Microsoft Store policies. Purchase-related information may be processed by Microsoft in accordance with Microsoft’s own privacy policy. ## 8. Basic plan usage counter If the Basic subscription includes a monthly camera usage limit, RTSP Bridge may store usage information in order to enforce that limit. The usage counter applies to active virtual camera streaming and not simply to the amount of time the application remains open. To help prevent abuse or circumvention of usage limits, usage information may be synchronized with a licensing system or service associated with the user’s subscription. Such usage information does not include camera video, RTSP passwords, or the content of the video stream. ## 9. Diagnostics and error reports RTSP Bridge does not automatically send complete logs, camera video, or camera credentials to the publisher. If the user chooses to report a problem, the user may voluntarily send diagnostic information by email to: **[tytusborycki@gmail.com](mailto:tytusborycki@gmail.com)** Before sending a support request, users should review its contents and avoid including passwords, access tokens, or other confidential information unless it is necessary to resolve the problem. ## 10. Purposes of processing Information processed by RTSP Bridge may be used to: * connect the application to selected cameras, * receive and process RTSP video, * create virtual camera devices in Windows, * store user configuration, * automatically reconnect to cameras, * discover cameras on the local network, * verify subscription status, * enforce limits associated with the selected subscription plan, * resolve technical issues reported by users, * maintain security and reliable operation of the application. ## 11. Legal bases for processing Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal data may be processed primarily: * to perform a contract and provide the functionality of the application, * to comply with legal obligations, * on the basis of legitimate interests, including security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and technical support, * on the basis of user consent where consent is required for a specific type of processing. ## 12. Sharing of information RTSP Bridge does not sell users’ personal information. Information may be shared with third parties only where necessary to provide a specific function or where required by law. This may include: * **Microsoft**, for Microsoft Store distribution, licensing, subscriptions, purchases, and payment processing, * technical service providers, if future versions of RTSP Bridge use infrastructure for licensing or usage-limit synchronization, * public authorities, where disclosure is required under applicable law. If RTSP Bridge begins using additional external services in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. ## 13. Data retention Configuration data, saved camera profiles, and camera credentials are stored locally for as long as the user keeps the relevant configuration or application installed. Users can remove saved devices from within RTSP Bridge. Subscription-related data is retained according to Microsoft Store policies. Information voluntarily provided in connection with technical support may be retained for as long as necessary to resolve the request, investigate recurring issues, defend against legal claims, or comply with legal obligations, and will be deleted when it is no longer needed. ## 14. Security RTSP Bridge uses technical measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access to stored information. These measures include the use of security features provided by Windows and limiting the amount of information transferred outside the user’s device. However, no information system can guarantee absolute security. Users should use strong camera passwords, keep camera firmware up to date, and secure their local network appropriately. ## 15.
